About the firm

Silver Fox Horizons is an international strategic advisory firm partnering with governments, institutions and businesses to strengthen state capacity, elevate global influence and unlock investment opportunities across Africa.

Kilimanjaro over the plainsAbout
Our mandate

We work at the intersection of statecraft, international positioning and investment diplomacy. Our purpose is simple: to help African states become stronger, better understood and better partnered.

Governments across the continent are pursuing ambitious agendas with real constraints: limited capacity at the centre, intense competition for attention and capital, and a global audience that often sees Africa through an outdated lens.

We bring together the disciplines needed to meet that moment: the craft of making government work, the communication that earns a nation its standing, and the diplomacy that turns interest into investment.

How we work

Six principlesbehind every mandate.

01

Strengthen, don’t replace

We build capability inside the institutions we serve, so progress lasts beyond our engagement.

02

Delivery over documents

Advice is judged by what changes. We favour practical routines and clear decisions over long reports.

03

Candour, with discretion

We tell leaders what they need to hear, and we protect the confidence placed in us.

04

Local insight, global view

We pair a close reading of national context with an understanding of how international partners and markets think.

05

Joined-up by design

Reform, reputation and investment are connected. We bring the three together rather than treating them in isolation.

06

Partnership over time

The most important work is rarely quick. We prefer long relationships built on trust and results.
