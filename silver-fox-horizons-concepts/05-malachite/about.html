Silver Fox Horizons was founded on a simple belief: that Africa’s influence in the world will be built by capable states, credible stories and partnerships of mutual respect.

Our mandate

Our mandate

To strengthen state capacity, elevate global influence and unlock investment opportunities across Africa.

Principles

How we conduct ourselves

Six commitments that govern every engagement, whoever the client and wherever the work.

I

Sovereignty first

We serve the priorities of the governments and institutions we work with. Our counsel strengthens their ownership; it never substitutes for it.

II

Discretion

Much of the most important work happens out of view. We hold confidences carefully and let our clients take the credit.

III

Evidence and judgement

We combine rigorous analysis with practical political judgement, because good advice must be both right and workable.

IV

Mutual interest

The partnerships that last are those in which every party gains. We design for durable alignment, not short-term advantage.

V

Integrity

We advise without fear or favour, decline work that conflicts with our values and say plainly what we think.

VI

Delivery

Strategy matters only when it is implemented. We stay close to the work until results are visible.

Approach

A disciplined method

Four movements, adapted to the institution and the moment.

01

Understand

Context before conclusions: institutions, interests and the political economy.

02

Frame

A clear strategy and narrative, with the choices it requires made explicit.

03

Convene

The right parties at the same table, prepared and aligned.

04

Deliver

Support through implementation, until results are visible.
