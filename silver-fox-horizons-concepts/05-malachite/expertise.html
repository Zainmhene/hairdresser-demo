Practice I

Statecraft, Governance & Institutional Transformation

Capable states are built deliberately. We work alongside leaders to turn ambition into institutions that perform.

What we do

Helping governments strengthen institutional capacity, design and implement reforms, and improve public-sector performance. We support governments and public institutions to set priorities, design reforms that fit their context and build the machinery to deliver them. Our role is to strengthen capacity that remains in place long after an engagement ends, never to replace it.

Typical engagements

  • Centre-of-government and delivery unit design
  • Reform strategy, sequencing and implementation planning
  • Institutional reviews and organisational redesign
  • Public-sector performance frameworks and routines
  • Leadership advisory for ministers and senior officials
  • Cabinet-level policy prioritisation and coordination

Who it is for

  • Heads of state’s offices
  • Ministries
  • Public agencies
  • Development institutions

Practice II

Nation Branding, Strategic Communications & Global Positioning

Every nation has a story. The question is who tells it, and whether the world believes it.

What we do

Supporting governments in strengthening their international reputation, soft power and global visibility. We help governments define a credible national narrative and project it with discipline: to investors, partners, multilateral forums and global media. Reputation is built on substance, so we connect the story to the policies and achievements behind it.

Typical engagements

  • National positioning strategy and narrative development
  • Strategic communications for reform programmes
  • International media and stakeholder engagement planning
  • Summit, state visit and major-event positioning
  • Reputation risk assessment and crisis preparedness
  • Soft power and cultural diplomacy strategy

Who it is for

  • Presidencies
  • Foreign ministries
  • Investment promotion agencies
  • Tourism and trade bodies

Practice III

Investment Diplomacy, Market Access & Strategic Partnerships

Capital follows confidence. We build the understanding and relationships that let serious investment happen.

What we do

Helping international companies and investors navigate African markets, engage with governments and develop strategic investment and commercial partnerships. We read the political and commercial landscape with our clients, prepare them to engage government with clarity and respect, and structure partnerships that serve both commercial and national interests. For governments, we help shape investment propositions that the market can act upon.

Typical engagements

  • Market entry strategy and political-economy briefings
  • Government engagement planning and high-level introductions
  • Partnership structuring between investors and the state
  • Investment proposition design for governments
  • Investor roadshow and forum preparation
  • Stakeholder mapping and ongoing relationship stewardship

Who it is for

  • International companies
  • Investors and funds
  • Development finance institutions
  • Governments seeking investment
