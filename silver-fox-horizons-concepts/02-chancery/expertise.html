I.
Practice I

Statecraft, Governance & Institutional Transformation

Governments rarely lack ambition. What they often lack is the institutional machinery to turn a programme for government into results that citizens can feel. We work alongside leaders at the centre of government and in line ministries to build that machinery, and to make it last.

We help define a small number of genuine priorities, design the reforms that serve them, and put in place the routines, data and people needed to deliver. We are equally comfortable in the cabinet room and the delivery unit, and we measure our contribution by what remains once we have gone.

Typical engagements

  • Centre-of-government and delivery unit design
  • Reform strategy, sequencing and implementation support
  • Institutional reviews and organisational redesign
  • Performance management and public-sector productivity
  • Strategic policy units and cabinet-level advisory
  • Leadership counsel for ministers and senior officials

Who it is for

  • Offices of heads of state and government
  • Ministries, departments and agencies
  • Public institutions and regulators
  • Development partners supporting reform

“Reform is not an announcement. It is a capability a state builds, and keeps.”

II.
Practice II

Nation Branding, Strategic Communications & Global Positioning

Every nation has a reputation, whether or not it chooses to shape it. Reputation influences where capital flows, where talent settles, which partnerships form and how a country’s voice is heard in the rooms that matter.

We help governments articulate a credible national narrative and carry it consistently: through policy, diplomacy, culture and communication. Our approach favours substance over slogans, and authorship at home over borrowed images. The goal is not visibility for its own sake, but influence that serves national interest.

Typical engagements

  • National narrative and reputation strategy
  • Soft-power and cultural diplomacy programmes
  • Strategic communications for presidencies and ministries
  • International media and stakeholder engagement
  • Positioning for summits, major events and bids
  • Issues and crisis counsel for governments

Who it is for

  • Presidencies and offices of heads of state
  • Ministries of foreign affairs, information and tourism
  • Investment and export promotion agencies
  • Cities and regional governments

“A nation’s reputation is a strategic asset. It deserves the same care as its reserves.”

III.
Practice III

Investment Diplomacy, Market Access & Strategic Partnerships

Africa’s markets reward those who arrive with patience, respect and an understanding of how decisions are really made. We help international companies and investors engage with governments as partners, and help governments engage with investors on equal terms.

We combine political-economy insight with a practical grasp of what investors and governments each need from a partnership. We prepare the ground, open the right conversations, and help structure relationships that hold up long after the signing ceremony.

Typical engagements

  • Market-entry strategy and political-economy analysis
  • Government engagement and stakeholder mapping
  • Structuring public–private and strategic partnerships
  • Preparation for investor and ministerial meetings
  • Investment promotion strategy for governments
  • Partnership governance and relationship stewardship

Who it is for

  • International companies entering or expanding in Africa
  • Institutional and strategic investors
  • Development finance institutions
  • Governments seeking credible, long-term partners

“Capital follows confidence. Confidence follows relationships handled with care.”
